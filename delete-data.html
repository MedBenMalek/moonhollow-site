Delete your data

How to delete your Moonhollow account and everything linked to it.

Delete your Moonhollow account

Send an email to benmalekchrif@gmail.com from the address you use with Moonhollow, with the subject "Delete my Moonhollow account".

If you signed in with Apple, Google, Facebook or X, also tell us which one, and the name shown on your Moonhollow profile.

We confirm the request and permanently delete your account within 30 days, including your name, email address and the information received from that service. A Plus subscription is managed by the App Store or Google Play: cancel it there so you aren't charged again.

A "Delete account" button in the app (Profile) is coming soon and will do the same in one step.

If you signed in with Facebook

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find Moonhollow and choose Remove. This stops Facebook sharing anything more with us.
  3. Then send us the email above, so we delete what we already have.

Data on your phone

Your settings, player lists and saved games are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling Moonhollow, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes them.

Online games

Names, choices and chat from online rooms and narrated tables are kept only while the room is open and are deleted when it closes. There's nothing more to delete.
