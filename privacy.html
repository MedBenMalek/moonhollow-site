Moonhollow

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Moonhollow is an app for playing the party game Werewolf, at a table with one phone as narrator or online with friends. It is made by Med Ben Malek ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Moonhollow apps for iPhone and Android and the Moonhollow website, why, and the choices you have.

In short: you can play without an account. If you create one, we keep your name and email address to sign you in. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

1. Playing without an account

  • On your device: your settings (language, sound, display), the names of the players you add, and the game in progress are saved on your phone only. They don't reach us.
  • Online rooms and narrated tables: to run a game, our game server receives the name you type, the room or table code, your choices during the game and the chat messages you send. It keeps them in memory only while the room is open and deletes them when it closes. We don't store a history of games or chats.

2. If you create an account

  • With an email and password: your name, email address and a password. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we can't read it.
  • With Apple, Google, Facebook or X: we receive the basic profile that service shares with your permission: your name, email address, a unique account ID and, for some services, a profile picture link. We never receive your password for those services. With Apple you may choose to hide your email; we then receive a relay address from Apple.
  • Account records: when the account was created and when you last signed in.

3. Moonhollow Plus

Moonhollow Plus is a subscription sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your card or payment details. We receive only whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock Plus.

4. Why we use this information

  • To run the game, online rooms and narrated tables.
  • To sign you in and keep your name, settings and Plus on every device you use.
  • To answer your messages and keep the service secure (for example, to stop abuse).

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our agreement with you (providing the game and your account) and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

5. Who else handles the data

  • Supabase: stores accounts and signs players in. Its servers are in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Our game server's hosting provider: runs online rooms and narrated tables.
  • Apple, Google, Meta (Facebook) and X: only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what you share with them.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: for downloads and Plus subscriptions.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account. We then delete it within 30 days.
  • Online game data: only while the room is open.
  • Data on your device: until you remove it or uninstall the app.

7. Your rights and choices

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, and you can object to how we use it. Write to benmalekchrif@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. To delete your account, follow these steps. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Moonhollow is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored hashed, and access to our systems is limited. No service is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.

11. Contact
